# Kenya Data Protection Act (DPA) Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Compliance Standard:** Kenya Data Protection Act  
**Effective Date:** October 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy ensures [Organization] complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act and respects individual data rights.

## 2. Legal Basis for Data Processing

Data may only be processed when there is a lawful basis:

1. **Consent** - Explicit, informed, and freely given consent
2. **Contract** - Necessary to perform contractual obligations
3. **Legal Obligation** - Required by law
4. **Vital Interests** - Necessary to protect life
5. **Public Task** - Official authority functions
6. **Legitimate Interests** - Organization's interests (proportionate)

## 3. Individual Rights (Article 14-27)

### 3.1 Right to Access
- Individuals may request access to their personal data
- Must provide copy within 30 days
- Format: Clear, intelligible, accessible
- No charge for first copy; reasonable fee for additional copies

### 3.2 Right to Correction
- Individuals may request inaccurate data be corrected
- Must act within 30 days
- Notify all recipients if shared

### 3.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
- Individuals may request deletion
- Must delete unless:
  - Legal obligation to retain
  - Necessary for processing
  - Public interest
- Notify recipients of deletion request

### 3.4 Right to Restrict Processing
- Individuals may limit how data is used
- Data retained but not actively processed
- Individuals notified of restrictions

### 3.5 Right to Data Portability
- Individual may obtain data in machine-readable format
- May transmit to another controller
- Does not affect data held for other purposes

### 3.6 Right to Object
- Individuals may object to processing
- Must stop processing unless:
  - Legal grounds override
  - Public interest
  - Legitimate interests

## 4. Data Subject Rights Procedures

### 4.1 Verification
- Verify requester identity
- Maintain proof of verification
- Document response and actions

### 4.2 Response Timeline
- Acknowledge receipt within 3 working days
- Provide substantive response within 30 days
- May extend by 60 days for complex requests
- Notify requester of extension

### 4.3 Handling Requests
- Assign owner for request
- Track in rights register
- Document all actions
- Maintain audit trail

## 5. Data Breach Notification

### 5.1 Mandatory Notification (72-hour requirement)
Notify Kenya DPA **without undue delay** and at latest within **72 hours** if breach poses risk to individuals.

### 5.2 Notification Content
- Description of breach
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken to mitigate
- Data Protection Officer contact

### 5.3 Individual Notification
Notify individuals without undue delay if high risk:
- Nature of breach
- Likely consequences
- Measures to mitigate harm
- Contact information

## 6. Data Protection Officer (DPO)

### 6.1 Appointment
- Appoint DPO if:
  - Public authority
  - Data processing is core activity
  - Processing at scale
- Independent, expert in data protection

### 6.2 Contact Information
Publish and share:
- Email: dpo@[organization].com
- Mailing address
- Available for data subject inquiries

## 7. Privacy Impact Assessment

Conduct assessment for:
- New processing systems
- Large-scale processing
- Sensitive data
- Automated decision-making

Assessment includes:
- Description of processing
- Necessity and proportionality
- Risk to individual privacy
- Mitigation measures

## 8. Data Processing Agreements

All processors must have:
- Written Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- Specific terms for:
  - Instructions for processing
  - Confidentiality obligations
  - Security measures
  - Sub-processor authorization
  - Assistance with rights requests
  - Assistance with obligations

## 9. International Data Transfers

For transfers outside Kenya:
- Recipient must have adequate protection
- Individual must be informed
- Documented decision on adequacy
- Standard contractual clauses recommended

## 10. Consent Management

### 10.1 Obtaining Consent
- Clear language, not in standard terms
- Separate from other matters
- Easy to withdraw
- Confirmation of consent (record)

### 10.2 Consent Records
- Maintain proof of consent
- When obtained
- What was consented to
- How was consent obtained

### 10.3 Withdrawing Consent
- Easy mechanism to withdraw
- No penalty for withdrawal
- Withdrawal as easy as giving
- Notify processors of withdrawal

## 11. Non-Compliance Penalties

**Administrative Fines:**
- Up to 5 million KES or 2% of annual revenue (whichever is higher) for:
  - Failure to report breach
  - Inadequate privacy notices
  - Lack of consent
  
- Up to 10 million KES or 4% of annual revenue for:
  - Violation of core principles
  - Lack of data protection measures
  - Failure to respond to rights requests

## 12. Compliance Monitoring

- Quarterly privacy audits
- Annual DPA compliance assessment
- Individual rights response tracking
- Breach register review
